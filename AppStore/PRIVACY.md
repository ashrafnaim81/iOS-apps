# Privacy Policy — Sudoku Santai

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Sudoku Santai ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. We respect your privacy.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- No accounts or sign-in
- No advertising and no ad networks
- No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs
- No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
- The app works fully offline and makes no network requests

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, statistics, streaks and achievements are saved only on your
device (in the app's local storage). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when
you delete the app.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
