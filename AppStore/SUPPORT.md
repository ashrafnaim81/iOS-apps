# Sudoku Santai — Support

Thanks for playing Sudoku Santai!

## How to play

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the numbers 1–9
exactly once. Tap a cell, then tap a number. Tap **Notes** to jot pencil marks.
You can replay the tutorial any time from **Settings → How to Play**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the app free?**
Yes. There are no ads and no accounts.

**Where is my progress saved?**
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**Why is there no sound?**
Sound effects and music follow your iPhone's silent switch. Music also stays off while other
audio is playing. Both can be switched on or off in **Settings**.

**Is the Daily Challenge the same for everyone?**
Yes. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day.
